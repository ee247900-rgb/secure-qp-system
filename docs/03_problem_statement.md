# Problem Statement

## Background
Government and competitive examinations are critical gateways for employment, higher education, and resource allocation. Millions of candidates appear for these exams, relying on a fair and meritocratic process. However, the integrity of these examinations relies heavily on the confidentiality of the question paper. 

## The Challenge
In recent years, the education sector has witnessed multiple severe incidents of question paper leakages. These leaks occur across various stages of the exam preparation and distribution cycle—ranging from the initial question setting, moderation, printing, transit, and storage at exam centers. 

Current systems often rely on physical security measures (e.g., sealed envelopes, police escorts, strongrooms) or outdated digital mechanisms lacking cryptographic enforcement. Once a leak occurs, tracing its origin is notoriously difficult due to poor audit trails and widespread collusion among malicious actors.

## Impact of Paper Leaks
1. **Financial Impact:** Re-conducting examinations costs governments and authorities millions of dollars in logistics, re-printing, and administrative overhead.
2. **Social Impact:** Genuine candidates suffer from anxiety, loss of trust in the system, and delays in academic or career progression. It fundamentally breaks the meritocratic structure of society.
3. **Legal Impact:** Prolonged court cases, public interest litigations, and investigations consume judicial bandwidth and administrative focus.

## Need for a Technological Solution
There is an urgent need for a robust, technology-driven solution that mathematically and cryptographically ensures the confidentiality and integrity of the question paper. We need a system that prevents any single individual (including system administrators) from accessing the complete question paper prematurely, provides a tamper-proof audit trail for every action, and automates the secure release of the paper exactly at the scheduled time.
